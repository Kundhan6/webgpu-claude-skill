## How to read this

- **Math** = computed with code and checked three ways.
  Dasha and Transit lines, all dates, and every "Math" box are math.
- **Meaning** = what old texts and good modern writers say.
  It is traditional meaning, not proven fact.
- Every reading has a confidence (high, medium or low) and a one-line reason.
- Codes like S4 point to the Sources list at the end.

Settings, in plain words:
- Sidereal zodiac = signs fixed to the stars.
- Lahiri ayanamsa = the standard Indian setting for that.
- Whole-sign houses = one sign is one house.

Terms, explained once:
- Lagna = rising sign (yours is Cancer).
- Dasha = planet period. Maha = main period, Antar = sub-period, Pratyantar = sub-sub-period.
- Transit = where a planet is in the sky now.
- Lord = the planet that owns a sign. "Rules the 2nd" = owns your 2nd house.
- Aspect = a planet "looks at" another house.
- Benefic = gentle planet. Malefic = harsh planet.
- Exalted = in its best sign. Debilitated = in its weakest sign.
- Yogakaraka = the single best planet for your rising sign.
- Yoga = a planet combination.
- BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Brihat Jataka and the rest are old astrology books.
  Full names are in Sources.

Birth time and dates:
- Your Lagna is safe. It stays Cancer for any birth time from 6:15 to 8:26 PM.
- Dasha dates are not that safe. They move about 2 months for every
  15 minutes the birth time is off. Read period edges as "around".

## 5. Acting window, Oct 2027 to 2029

**Math**
- Periods: Jupiter–Venus to 7 May 2028. Then Jupiter–Sun to 23 Feb 2029.
  Then Jupiter–Moon to 25 Jun 2030. Each edge can move about ±2 months.
- Saturn in Aries (your 10th from Lagna): 23 Feb 2028 – 8 Aug 2029.
- Saturn enters Taurus on 8 Aug 2029; that starts Sade Sati (Saturn's 7.5-year pass over your Moon).
  It is back in Aries 5 Oct 2029 – 17 Apr 2030.
- Jupiter returns to its birth degree on 26 Oct 2027, 10 Apr 2028 and 17 Jun 2028.
- Jupiter is in Libra (5th from your Moon) 26 Dec 2028 – 29 Mar 2029.
- Saturn and Jupiter both touch your 10th from Lagna (double transit):
  - 28 Feb – 24 Jul 2028
  - 26 Dec 2028 – 29 Mar 2029
  - 5 Oct 2029 – 25 Jan 2030
- Rahu enters Sagittarius on 24 Jun 2028 (6th from Lagna, 7th from Moon).

**A. Jupiter–Sun (May 2028 – Feb 2029) as the main opening.** — Confidence: low
- Your Sun sits in the 6th, the house of contests, and Jupiter aspects it.
- B.V. Raman: a Jupiter-aspected 6th-house Sun brings success and promotion in its own sub-period.
- BPHS and Phaladeepika give honour and fame here. Other verses warn of opposition and loss or change of position.
- Read it as recognition through contest: auditions, rivals, proving yourself.
- It lines up with your plan to start acting in about two years. That is context, not proof.
- Why: Texts split two against two, and the edges move about 2 months.
- Sources: S17, S1 (BPHS 56.52), S2 (BPHS 56.54-55), S6 (21.44), S5 (20.28)

**B. Jupiter–Moon (from Feb 2029) brings your public face.** — Confidence: low
- The Moon rules your Lagna, so texts say you as a person come forward.
- One modern writer ties a 12th-house Moon to film.
- But BPHS reads this sub-period as unsettled for a 12th-house Moon with Saturn, and Sade Sati begins in Aug 2029.
- Why: Texts split, and the film link is one modern source.
- Sources: S1 (BPHS 48.2, 56.61-63), S6 (21.45), S20, S4 (v. 23)

**C. A later bloom, after 2030.** — Confidence: low
- BPHS gives the Jupiter–Rahu sub-period (Jun 2031 – Oct 2033) authority and meetings with people from abroad.
- Uttara Kalamrita makes Rahu's periods auspicious for your chart.
- Why: Two texts support it and their conditions are met, but it is far off.
- Sources: S2 (BPHS 56.72-75), S14 (UK II.82)

**Windows, best first**
1. 26 Dec 2028 – 29 Mar 2029: the best transits. Jupiter is good from your Moon and looks at your 10th while Saturn sits there.
   It spans the Sun-to-Moon handover around 23 Feb 2029. Medium for the transits; low for acting specifically.
2. 7 May 2028 – 23 Feb 2029: Jupiter–Sun. Recognition through contest, but texts also warn of opposition. Low.
3. 28 Feb – 24 Jul 2028: career houses stirred, plus the Jupiter return (Apr and Jun 2028). Low.
4. 26 Oct 2027 – 23 Feb 2028: a fresh-start point (Jupiter return), but credit comes slowly. Low.
5. From 8 Aug 2029: Sade Sati begins. Texts favour steady work over big launches.
   A Tamil tradition calls a second round "Pongu" (fruitful). You were born inside one, so this may be your second.
   No source rules on whether a round running at birth counts. Low.
- Sources: S4 (v. 2, 23), S19, S17, S18

**Bottom line:** if acting opens in this span, a classical Jupiter transit (5th from your Moon)
and the modern double transit point most to **26 Dec 2028 – 29 Mar 2029**.
- Confidence: medium for the transit window; low for acting as such.
- Why: The transit rule is classical with fixed dates, but the running period is contested and Saturn is weak in Aries.
