# Company names — round 3 (relevant to the product)

## 1. Assumptions

1. The new rule overrides round 2's "not locked to voice": every name must suggest answering, listening, the front desk or speech. Banned roots (Voice, Call, Ring, Talk, Chat, Echo…) and the phone-spelling rules still apply.
2. The names are suggestive, not literal. Literal names like "VoiceDesk" can't be trademarked in classes 9 and 42, and competitors already hold them.
3. No round-1 or round-2 name is reused.
4. .com: one RDAP loop, with a nonsense-domain control inside it, which returned 404. Fallback domains were not looked up: `get<name>.com` for greetings, verbs and phrases, `<name>hq.com` for roles and sounds.
5. Collision results come from one web-search snippet per name, not a trademark or MCA search. Flags marked "known:" come from my memory and were not searched.

The two survivors get their relevance from an idiom or phrase ("beck and call", "on hand"), not from the word itself. That's the pattern to follow.

## 4. Top 3 and the #1 pick

1. **Beck** — *"At your beck and call, 24/7."*
2. **Onhand** — *"Always on hand: every call, lead and follow-up."*
3. **Deskbell** — *"Ding. Every customer answered."* (unchecked)

### #1 pick: **Beck**

"At your beck and call" means *always ready to serve, the moment you ask*. That's the receptionist promise in plain English. The word itself doesn't describe voice or calls, so it can still be trademarked, and it stretches to the whole ecosystem: calls, WhatsApp, leads and posts are all "at your beck and call".

- It's one syllable and four letters.
- "Our business runs on Beck" works.
- "Beck Technologies Private Limited" reads cleanly.
- It has no bad meaning in Hindi, Telugu or Tamil.

**Risks to clear before filing:**
- BECK.Ai, a small AI content-creation tool that overlaps your marketing-posts feature;
- C.H.BECK's legal AI, which matters if you expand to the EU;
- Beck's beer (class 32, not yours);
- some callers may write "Bek", so buy that domain too.

**Next steps:**
1. MCA RUN check for "Beck Technologies Private Limited".
2. IP India search for "BECK" in classes 9, 35, 38 and 42.
3. Check availability of getbeck.com, beckhq.com, beck.ai and beck.in.
4. Spend the next 12 searches on the 13 unchecked names before dropping the backups.

If Beck fails, go with **Onhand**.
