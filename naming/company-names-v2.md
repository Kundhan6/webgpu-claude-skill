# Company names — round 2

## 1. Assumptions

1. No round-1 PDF was attached, so I only avoided the round-1 names in the brief (Kelvano, Delvora, Dorvan, Towerline, Watchkeep, Shipshape).
2. Phone test: a common word with its normal spelling passes (Slack's "ck" is fine). No name has v, w, z, ph, gh, kn, wr or mb. Flags cover ea/ee, -ar/-er and names that sound like Indian first names.
3. A real English compound (Upshot, Nonstop) counts as "two short words joined". Laudatory words (Solid, Flagship) are allowed, but they score lower on ownability.
4. .com: one RDAP loop, plus one control request on a nonsense domain, which returned 404, so the 200s are genuine. Fallback domains were not looked up. I used `<name>hq.com` for nouns (HQ also says "command centre") and `get<name>.com` for verbs and compounds.
5. Collision results come from one web-search snippet per name, not a trademark or MCA search. Flags marked "known:" come from my memory and were not searched.

## 4. Top 3 and the #1 pick

1. **Brass** — *"Top brass for every business, on duty 24/7."*
2. **Denim** — *"The everyday fabric your business runs on."*
3. **Braid** — *"Calls, chats, email and leads, woven into one."*

### #1 pick: **Brass**

Denim wins the raw score by one point, on phone spelling and ownability. Brass is the only finalist whose meaning says *command* without describing the product: "top brass" are the people who run the show. That matches "the command centre that runs a business, not a voice bot" (fit 5 vs Denim's 3).

- It's one syllable and five letters, and Indian callers already know the word (brass = *peetal*).
- It has no bad meaning in Hindi, Telugu or Tamil.
- "Our business runs on Brass" works as a sentence.
- "Brass Technologies Private Limited" reads cleanly, and brasshq.com repeats the HQ idea.

**Risks to clear before filing:**
- the "bras" typo, so also buy the misspelt domain;
- the UK bank named Brass (financial services, class 36, not 9/35/38/42, but confirm);
- the Jamnagar "Brass Software" shop (check that it isn't an MCA-registered name).

**Next steps:**
1. MCA RUN name check for "Brass Technologies Private Limited".
2. IP India public search for "BRASS" in classes 9, 35, 38 and 42.
3. Confirm brasshq.com, getbrass.com, brass.ai and brass.in availability.

If Brass fails any of these, go with **Denim**.
