# Company name candidates: replacing "Vocentra"

Prepared 24 Sep 2026 for Krish (Hyderabad). A B2B AI business platform with five pillars (voice, workflows, email, lead gen, marketing) and a "command centre" positioning. It launches in India first, then goes global, and must be registrable as a Pvt Ltd with MCA and trademarkable.

---

## 1. Assumptions

1. **Phone test.** I judged this for Indian-English callers. The flags mark the usual Indian-English spelling splits: v/w, z/j, c/k, t/th, i/ee, a/aa and silent h. I avoided Z entirely, because many Indian speakers say it as "j".
2. **Meaning checks.** I checked Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali and English slang from my own knowledge. No native speaker has verified them. "No flag" means I know of no problem, not that there is none. Have native speakers confirm the Top 3 (see §5).
3. **Collision checks.** Only 3 of the 150 names were web-searched: Helmora, Samast and Kelvano. That was the 6-search budget, and 3 searches went on the competitor list. Every other brand flag comes from my own knowledge. **No name is cleared until you run the §5 checks.**
4. **Domains (Step 3) were skipped.** The sandbox has no `dig`, `nslookup` or `host`, and per your instruction nothing was installed. The `.com` and `.in` columns show "—" (not checked). §5 has a PowerShell one-liner you can run on your Windows machine.
5. **Competitor facts** come from Sept 2026 search snippets plus background knowledge. I didn't read any pages in full (0 of 2 fetches used).
6. **MCA fit** is judged against the principle in Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, Rule 8. That rule rejects names identical or too similar to existing companies or registered trademarks, and names that imply government patronage or a regulated activity. This is not legal advice.
7. **Trademark plausibility** for classes 9/35/38/42 is a distinctiveness judgement only: coined names beat suggestive ones, which beat descriptive ones.
8. **Syllables** are counted as the name is spoken in Indian English. The style buckets overlap (some coined names have Indian roots), but each name is listed once.

Pattern tally across the 39: **13 are locked to voice, calls or chat** (Bol-, Ring, Call, Voice, -tel, -phore, Chat, Gupshup). There are 7 coined names, 5 Sanskrit/Hindi, 3 deliberate misspellings and 0 founder names. Only Palantir carries mythic gravitas.

### (a) Overused patterns and roots to AVOID
- **Voice, speech and call roots:** Voc-, Vox, Voice, Bol-, Vani/Vaani, Swar-, Katha-, Samvad, Ring, Call, Talk, Echo, Chat, Gup, -tel, -phone, -phore.
- **"Knowledge" or "all" roots already taken:** Gnani/Gyan/Jnana, Sarv-/Sarva (and synonyms like *samast*), Vidya, Medha, Mind, Sense/Sensy.
- **AI tropes:** -ify, -ly, -io, "AI" suffix, Synth, Neura/Neuro, Nexus, Cortex, Aura, Genie, Bot, Prime, Agent, -verse, -ix/-yx (Nurix), Flow, Loop, Stack, Hub, Box, Omni, Nova, Zen, Pulse, Fresh, -works.
- **Animal + tech compounds** (BotPenguin, HuskyVoice) and **deliberate misspellings** (Ringg, Haptik, Interakt). Both fail the phone test.
- **Tolkien names** (Palantir, Anduril, Mithril, Erebor), which are already owned by Palantir's orbit.
- **Indian words that sound like government schemes or portals:** Setu, Sahay, Saathi, Sampark, Sanchar, Kendra, Udyam, Niti, Shasan, Pradhan, Mantri, Kavach, Pragati, Atal, Param, Vahan, Nipun, Sugam, Saral/Sahaj (tax forms), Nidhi (a regulated company type), Bharat. Plus MCA's sensitive words: National, Bank, Insurance, Board, Commission, Authority, Union, Central.
- **Famous Indian brand roots:** Vantara, Vistara, Tejas, Druva, Kalpataru, Kite (Zerodha).

### (b) White space nobody owns
1. **Control of the whole business.** Every competitor names either a channel (voice, chat, call, WhatsApp) or a trait (knowledge, sense, speed). No Indian SMB player owns the vocabulary of *command and stewardship*: helm, reins, watch, tower, keel. The same goes for the Indian words for directing and arranging: *dor*, *sutradhar*, *samanvay*, *nirdesh*.
2. **Palantir-style gravitas at SMB scale.** In this category the serious, mythic register is empty below enterprise level. Indian SMB tools all sound cute (penguins, huskies, gupshup).
3. **A coined, plosive, 3-syllable name with a neutral sound in both India and globally.** Only Zoho and Plivo take the coined route, and both are 2 syllables and dev- or suite-flavoured.
4. **US analogues to keep clear of:** GoHighLevel, Podium and Weave are all-in-one SMB platforms with AI calling. Avoid "level", "podium" and "weave".

## 5. Manual checklist for the Top 3 (Kelvano, Towerline, Dorvan)

Do these in order. Stop on any hard hit and move to the next name.

**1. MCA name availability** (free, about 10 minutes)
- [ ] MCA portal → *MCA Services → Check Company/LLP Name*. Search each name **and its phonetic variants**, because the Registrar rejects "phonetically similar" names:
  - Kelvano: Kelvano, Kelvana, Calvano, Kelveno
  - Towerline: Towerline, Tower Line, Towerlines
  - Dorvan: Dorvan, Dorwan, Dorvaan, Dorven
- [ ] Also search the zaubacorp or tofler company search for the same strings. They catch LLPs and recently struck-off names.
- [ ] If clear, reserve through **SPICe+ Part A** (up to 2 names) or **RUN**. Use a full form such as "Kelvano Technologies Private Limited".

**2. IP India trademark public search** (free)
- [ ] ipindiaonline.gov.in → *Trade Marks → Public Search* → *Wordmark*. Run the "Start with", "Contains" **and "Phonetic"** searches in **classes 9, 35, 38, 42**.
- [ ] Check WIPO Global Brand Database and USPTO Trademark Search too, since you'll sell globally.
- [ ] If clear, file TM-A in all 4 classes. The e-filing fee for a startup/MSME/individual is about ₹4,500 per class (verify the current fee). Filing gives you a priority date immediately.

**3. Domains**
- [ ] Buy `.com`, `.in`, `.ai` and `.co.in`, plus the main misspelling (e.g. calvano / dorwan) to redirect.
- [ ] Quick signal first, from Windows PowerShell:
  ```powershell
  foreach ($n in "kelvano","towerline","dorvan") { foreach ($t in "com","in","ai") { $d="$n.$t"; try { Resolve-DnsName $d -Type NS -ErrorAction Stop | Out-Null; "$d  taken" } catch { "$d  likely free" } } }
  ```
- [ ] If the `.com` is parked or for sale, get a broker quote before you commit to the name.

**4. Social handles**
- [ ] Instagram `@name` (and `@name.ai` / `@name.hq` as a fallback)
- [ ] LinkedIn company page: `linkedin.com/company/name`
- [ ] X, YouTube, GitHub org. The WhatsApp Business display name needs Meta approval and must match the brand.

**5. Human checks (cheap and decisive)**
- [ ] **The real phone test.** Call 5 clinic, salon or real-estate owners, say the name once, and ask them to WhatsApp you the spelling.
- [ ] Ask native speakers of Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi and Bengali about any slang or awkward meaning. This is especially worth doing for Dorvan (Telugu *dora*).
- [ ] Google the name + "company", "app" and "AI" and scan the first 3 pages.
